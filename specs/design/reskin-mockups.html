Change hotkey
How to use it
  1. 01Highlight the text you want to change with your cursor.
  2. 02Hold ⌃+🌐 and say the change out loud.
  3. 03Remix rewrites it in place. Undo brings it back if you change your mind.

No highlight? Remix reads the whole field you're in. It can also search the web for facts and sources, and paste images straight into your draft.

Everyday writing

"Make this warmer and half as long — I sound like a lawyer."

"I wrote this angry. Make it firm but polite."

"Punch this up and get it under 280 characters."

"Turn these bullet points into a paragraph for the report."

"Turn this into Spanish, but keep my sign-off in English."

Work & school

"Convert this essay's citations to MLA format."

"Rewrite this contract clause in plain English a client can understand."

"Rewrite this commit message to explain why, not what."

"Turn these messy notes into a tidy bullet list, grouped by topic."

With the web

"Fact-check this against WebMD and flag anything that's off."

"Add a fun fact about espresso, and a picture of a pour-over."

Good to know

Highlight a sentence first to remix just that part — with nothing selected, Remix reads the whole field.

Tap the hotkey instead of holding it for one-tap quick routes (Control+1–3).

Not quite right? Type a follow-up in the chat card — it keeps the thread.
